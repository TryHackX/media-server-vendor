Ports of Winamp AVS plug-ins without published source, for this site's AVS visualizer.

What: TypeScript ports of APE plug-ins (and of the Sonique visualization plug-ins AVS ran through
its SVP Loader) that presets in vendor/avs-presets use, written by static reading of the plug-in
binaries as they came in the preset packs of the VISBOT archive (https://visbot.net/archive,
files.visbot.net/archive/<author>/<pack>), retrieved 2026-11-09.

How: the owner approved extracting the plug-in files from the packs' installers as data and
reading them statically (MSVC dumpbin, Python with -I). Nothing was run, loaded or emulated. The
binaries were deleted afterwards; the analysis notes are in scratch/avs-reference/apes/*.md.

Licence: the plug-ins' authors published no source and no licence for these binaries, so the
ports stay out of git (frontend/vendor/ is git-ignored). Credit for the effects belongs to them:
