Cthugha-4-Winamp 1.50 (1998), a visualization plug-in for Winamp
Krunoslav Pisacic, kpisacic@diana.zesoi.fer.hr, http://islands.zesoi.fer.hr/~kpisacic/cthugha

Ported for "Klasyka retro" by static analysis of the owner's copy of the plug-in
(vis_sh15.zip, SHA-256 69b9af912cf3f4abf9605fb1acfd0d2f88db184edaf28d7bb9b414f361a85afe:
Plugins/Vis_sh.dll and its maps, objs, pics; the tab generators' sources from tabgens_src.zip),
and of the three generators in its tabs folder that came without C source: Wintabs.exe (with
wintabs.bas), Wintab.exe and TABMAKE.EXE (Wintabs.zip, Wintab.zip, tabmake.zip). Nothing of
it was run; see SOURCE.md.
